Why plants choose Punt.

Six problems production managers know. What we do about them, and what you will see.

The problemWhat Punt doesWhat you get

Less waiting

The problemThe line stands still while one scale or one hopper catches up.

What Punt doesDosing units run independently, each batch moves to the next free point on its route, and after a fault dosing resumes from the line it stopped on.

What you getFewer idle minutes between batches, visible in the transition and waiting reports.See it on the screen

Losses with a cause

The problemA short shift or a drifting batch, and nobody can say why.

What Punt doesEvery transition and wait, alarm, operator command and target-versus-actual weighing is recorded with time and source.

What you getYou see where the time and material went, and who or what was involved.See it on the screen

No mixed material

The problemA wrong route or silo can mix materials and batches.

What Punt doesRoute and equipment locks block a route that is already in use. Lots and stock are tracked from intake to packing, and every bag is recorded.

What you getMaterial ends up where it should, with a record that proves it.See it on the screen

03DosingEvery dosing unit weighs its own share of the batch on its own scale.
Units do not wait for each other

One material, two silos
Draw the same raw material from two silos, one after the other by percentage or at the same time.
When a silo runs empty
A flow fault alarm is raised. The operator switches the line to another silo with the same material and enough stock, or moves the line to the end of the batch.
Move a line to another scale
A line can be handed to another dosing unit’s scale to finish the batch.
Cut-off value per silo
Each silo keeps its own cut-off value, so the feeder stops at the right moment for that material.

Questions before we talk.

Do you only work with feed mills?

No. Our SCADA product is built for feed mills, and that is where our process depth is. For other industrial production lines we engineer PLC, SCADA and field automation as a project scoped to the line.

Can we monitor the plant remotely?

Punt SCADA runs in a web browser. Authorized users can follow production, alarms and reports on your plant network, or remotely over a secure connection you set up. Each person has an account and role. Remote access does not replace someone on site for physical faults.

Will it work with my existing PLC?

We need to review your controllers, line structure and the signals you want to use. Bring the brands, models and current setup to our conversation and we will clarify the scope.

Will installation interrupt production?

That depends on the changes involved and your line. We plan commissioning with you around your production schedule.

Can you tell us how much we will save?

Not before we have measured your line. We can show you which figures to measure and how Punt records them.

Are the screens on this site live?

No. They are screenshots of the Punt SCADA product. They do not control a real facility.
