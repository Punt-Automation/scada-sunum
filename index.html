From a packing order back to the raw material lots.

Pick a step. Each one is a real Punt SCADA record; the examples come from different batches, and the links work the same way for each lot.

Which finished lots went into this packing order?

The packing record lists the finished lots it used, with kilograms from each, and each bag weighed by the connected packing scales, with time, scale and weight.

Packing and dosing allocate lots first-in, first-out from silo stock records, so the links are as accurate as the stock records in the silos. A raw material lot links to its intake record when the material arrived through a Punt intake transfer.

What is one hour of waiting worth in your plant?

Your own figures, no percentages and no promises. Just your average output per hour, so you can weigh what a waiting report shows.

Your average output per hour
—
daily production ÷ operating hours
One hour of waiting per day is worth
—
output per hour × operating days
Ten minutes of waiting per day is worth
—
one hour ÷ 6

Enter all three figures to see the result.

Keep in mind. Waiting only turns into extra output if the line itself is the limit, not demand, raw material or another station.

Punt’s waiting, transition and production reports are where you measure this, before and after a change.

Feed mill depth, station by station.

The features of our feed mill SCADA, in the order material moves. Open a station for details.

01Raw material intakeFrom the intake pit to the right silo, with the route set from one screen.
One click to start
Pick the source and target silo, then start. Punt writes the route to the PLC and logs the transfer with its lot.
Feeder follows the elevator
The intake feeder speed is regulated against the elevator motor current setpoint.
Spare silo when the first one fills
Choose a spare target. When the primary silo is full, intake continues into the spare silo automatically.

Raw material intake

02Recipe and planningRecipes from your ration program, run as a series of batches.
Recipes from a TXT file
Drop the TXT export of your ration program, review the preview and approve. Products and formulas are created, and missing raw materials can be created from their codes.
Plan once, run the series
Set product, batch size, batch count and target silo. After one start, each dosing unit receives the next batch from the plan whenever its scale is empty.
Routing per raw material
Each raw material is assigned to a dosing unit and silo. One material can be split across two silos.
Orders to batches
Enter customer orders, plan batches against them and follow planned, produced and remaining kilograms. The order report links each order to its batches and product lots.

Production plan

03DosingEach dosing unit weighs its own share of the batch on its own scale.
Units do not wait for each other
When a unit’s scale is empty, the PLC asks for its next batch and Punt sends it. A premix scale can take the next batch while the main scale is still weighing.
One material, two silos
Draw the same raw material from two silos, one after the other by percentage or at the same time.
When a silo runs empty
A flow fault alarm is raised. The operator switches the line to another silo with the same material and enough stock, or moves the line to the end of the batch.
Move a line to another scale
A line can be handed to another dosing unit’s scale to finish the batch.
Pause and resume
Dosing can be paused and resumed. After a fault is cleared, it continues from the line that was being weighed.
Manual dosing on record
Manual dosing at the PLC panel is detected and reported separately, so it does not disappear into the automatic weighing.
Cut-off value per silo
Each silo keeps its own cut-off value, so the feeder stops at the right moment for that material.

Dosing

04Production and transferEach batch from the scale through the mill and mixer to the product silo.
The line on one screen
Live weights, motor currents, mixer status, premix additions and the batch number at each hopper.
Batches stay separate
A batch moves to the next free hopper on its path rather than into the batch ahead. This sequencing runs in our PLC application.
Finished product to the planned silo
The product silo set in the plan receives its fill number automatically. Stock and lot are booked once the PLC confirms the fill.

Production line

05PelletingThe press from the product silo to the pellet silo, with a record of each work order.
Automatic feeder and steam
Feeder speed and steam valve are controlled automatically within the limits set on the press parameter panel.
Work orders with lots
Each press work order records capacity, current, temperature, feeder and valve averages, downtime and the lots it consumed.

Pellet press

06PackingPunt communicates with the packing scales and records each bag they weigh.
Bag limit closes the gate
Set a bag limit for the silo. When it is reached, the silo gate closes automatically.
Settings kept per silo
Slow-down and cut-off values are saved for each product silo, so each feed keeps its own settings.

Questions before we talk.

Do you only work with feed mills?

No. Our SCADA product is built for feed mills, and that is where our process depth is. For other industrial production lines we engineer PLC, SCADA and field automation as a project scoped to the line.

Can we monitor and operate the plant remotely?

Punt SCADA runs in a web browser. Over a secure connection you provide, authorized users see the same screens and can use the functions their role allows. With your permission, our support team can look at the same screens to help. Remote access does not replace someone on site for mechanical or electrical faults.

Will it work with my existing PLC?

We need to review your controllers, line structure and the signals you want to use. Bring the brands, models and current setup to our conversation and we will clarify the scope.

Will installation interrupt production?

That depends on the changes involved and your line. We plan commissioning with you around your production schedule.

Can you tell us how much we will save?

Not before we have measured your line. We can show you which figures to measure and how Punt records them.

Are the screens on this site live?

No. They are screenshots of the Punt SCADA product. They do not control a real facility.
