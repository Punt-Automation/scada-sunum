Raw material intake

Bring material in from the intake pit to the right silo, with the route set from one screen.

Raw material intake

One click to start
Pick the source and target silo, then start. Punt writes the route to the PLC and logs the transfer with its lot.
Feeder follows the elevator
The intake feeder speed is regulated against the elevator motor current setpoint, so the elevator is loaded without being overloaded.
Spare silo when the first one fills
Choose a spare target. When the primary silo is full, intake continues into the spare silo automatically.
No shared-equipment clashes
A route that uses a conveyor already in use by another transfer cannot be selected.
02

Recipe and planning

Load recipes from your ration program and turn them into batches with one start.

Production plan

Recipes from a TXT file
Drop the TXT export of your ration program. Review the preview, then approve: products and formulas are created, and missing raw materials can be created from their codes.
Plan once, run the series
Set the product, batch size, batch count and target silo. One start runs the requested batches through to the semi-finished product silo.
Routing per raw material
Each raw material is assigned to a dosing unit and silo. One material can be split across two silos.
03

Dosing

Every dosing unit weighs its own share of the batch on its own scale.

Independent dosing units

Units do not wait for each other
Each dosing unit runs independently. A premix scale can finish and take the next batch while the main scale is still weighing the current one.
One material, two silos
Draw the same raw material from two silos, one after the other by percentage or at the same time.
When a silo runs empty
A flow fault alarm is raised. The operator switches the line to another silo that holds the same material and enough stock, or moves the line to the end of the batch. The batch is not cancelled.
Move a line to another scale
A line can be handed to another dosing unit's scale to finish the batch.
Resume where it stopped
Pause, clear the fault and resume. Dosing continues from the line that was being weighed.

AUTO CUTOFF learns from each batch

Material keeps falling after the feeder stops. The cut-off value tells the PLC how early to stop. Punt corrects it from real weighings, only where you allow it.

  1. A batch finishes and the PLC reports it complete.
  2. For each line with AUTO CUTOFF on, the net weight is compared with the target.
  3. If the error is outside that silo's tolerance, the cut-off value is corrected by half of the error.
  4. The new value is written to the PLC for the next batch.

Lines with AUTO CUTOFF off are never changed. Skipped lines, lines that did not reach their cut-off point and manually closed batches do not teach the system.

04

Production and transfer

Follow each batch from the scale through the mill and mixer to the product silo.

Production line

Next free hopper, no mixing
A batch moves on as soon as the next hopper on its path is free. Batches stay separate, so one feed does not run into another.
Every step on one screen
Live weights, motor currents, mixer status, premix additions and the batch number at each hopper.
Where time is lost
Transition and waiting reports show how long each batch spent between stations, and where it waited.
05

Pelleting

Run the press from the product silo to the pellet silo, and keep the record of each work order.

Pellet press

Automatic feeder and steam
Feeder speed and steam valve are controlled automatically within the limits you set on the press parameter panel.
Press statistics while it runs
Current, feeder, temperature, valve opening, average capacity, processed tonnage and remaining time.
Work orders with lots
Each press work order records its averages, downtime, target silo and the product lots it consumed.
06

Packing

Punt talks to the packing scales and records every bag.

Packing

Bag-level report
Every bag is recorded with its time, scale and weight, together with the finished lots it came from.
Settings kept per silo
Slow-down and cut-off values are saved for each product silo, so each feed keeps its own settings.
Bag limit closes the gate
Set a bag limit for the silo. When it is reached, the silo gate closes automatically.
07

Traceability and reports

From any finished lot back to the raw material lots it used, and forward to the press and the bag.

Lot report: overview

One lot, the whole story
Formula, actual weights per dosing line, raw material lots, press work order and packing work order for a single lot.
Lot and stock tracking
Silo stock movements and consumption by lot, from intake to packing.
Operator activity and alarms
Every command is logged with the user who sent it. Alarm history and analog trends show what happened and when.
Permissions per person
Each person signs in with their own account and role, and page access can be limited user by user.
