Where mills lose, and what you get back.

Less waiting between batches

Idle equipment between batches is capacity you already paid for. Punt keeps the line moving where the process allows it.

Dosing units do not wait for each other
Each unit weighs its share on its own scale. A premix scale can take the next batch while the main scale is still weighing.
Next free hopper
A batch moves on as soon as the next hopper on its path is free, and batches stay separate.
Resume after a fault
Pause, clear the fault and resume. Dosing continues from the line that was being weighed.

Independent dosing units

The right material, in the right amount

Most material losses start with a wrong silo, a mixed batch or a weighing that drifts unnoticed.

Routes that cannot clash
A transfer route that uses a conveyor already taken by another transfer cannot be selected.
Weighing deviation per line
Target, actual and difference are recorded for every dosing line of every batch.
Bag-level packing records
Every bag is logged with time, scale and weight. Slow-down and cut-off values are kept per product silo.

Size the opportunity with your own numbers.

This is not a forecast and not a promise. Enter figures from your own records to see what a loss is worth to you. Punt’s reports are where you would measure these figures, and later check them again.

Nothing you enter leaves your browser.

How much feed do you give away above the declared bag weight?

Annual value with your figures—

Enter a number of zero or more in every field (recovered share up to 100%).

Formula
bags per year × overfill (kg per bag) × feed cost per kg
Keep in mind
Only the part of the overfill that a tighter cut-off can safely remove is recoverable. Legal and customer tolerances still apply.

03DosingEvery dosing unit weighs its own share of the batch on its own scale.
One material, two silos
Draw the same raw material from two silos, one after the other by percentage or at the same time.
When a silo runs empty
A flow fault alarm is raised. The operator switches the line to another silo with the same material and enough stock, or moves the line to the end of the batch.
Move a line to another scale
A line can be handed to another dosing unit’s scale to finish the batch.
Cut-off value per silo
Each silo keeps its own cut-off value, so the feeder stops at the right moment for that material.
