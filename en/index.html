Which production processes does the platform cover?

Production planning, SCADA floor monitoring, dosing, lot traceability and reporting. Explore example screens in the Product Atlas.

Will it work with my existing PLC or ERP?

We need to review your systems and the data that needs to be shared. Bring the brands, models and existing connection details to your discussion so we can clarify the scope.

Will installation interrupt production?

That depends on the changes involved and your existing line. Let’s review commissioning and its effect on production in the context of your factory.

What information is needed for a proposal?

Your facility type, existing system and main problem are a useful starting point. We can discuss software, equipment, reporting and training requirements together.

Are these screens connected to a live factory?

This website shows images of product screens. Selecting them does not control a real facility. We can discuss your own production flow in a meeting.

What happens after I request a meeting?

The form creates a meeting request. We will contact you to discuss your needs and arrange a time. Submitting the form does not confirm an appointment or a proposal.
